### 🏆 Social Impact Innovation

#### 🩺 Global Health: [pregAthI](https://github.com/ahkharsha/pregAthI)
**Dual-Mode Maternal Safety System** | *Embedded AI, IoT, Cellular*
- **Challenge**: Conventional devices fail without smartphones, leaving pregnant women vulnerable during connection drops.
- **Solution**: A dual-wearable ecosystem where the pendant switches to an autonomous **Edge AI Gateway** when phone connectivity fails, processing vitals locally to trigger cellular SOS alerts.
- **Impact**: Grand Finalist of **IEEE YESIST12 (Tunisia)** & **SmartIDEAthon 2024**; Shortlisted for **MSME 5.0**; Patent Filed.

#### 🏥 Health Security: [PolyMed](https://github.com/ahkharsha/PolyMed)
**Blockchain Healthcare Platform** | *Next.js, Solidity, ZK-Proofs*
- **Challenge**: Centralized databases are vulnerable to breaches; patients lack control over sensitive history.
- **Solution**: Architecture using **Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)** for identity, granting patients sovereignty over their records.
- **Impact**: Awarded **DevCon 2024 Bangkok Travel Scholarship** (Top 1% of global applicants).

#### 🌾 Economic Security: Quantum-Enhanced Agri-Ledger (QAL)
**Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning** | *Federated Learning, Blockchain*
- **Challenge**: Smallholder farmers lack data sovereignty and verifiable proof of sustainable practices.
- **Solution**: A privacy-preserving **Federated Learning model** that detects crop stress while securing farmer data ownership, allowing them to monetize data without compromising privacy.
- **Impact**: Research framework submitted to **IEEE Access**.
